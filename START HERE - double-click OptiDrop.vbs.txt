OptiDrop - built by Floki
=========================

To start: double-click  OptiDrop.vbs  in this folder.

That's it. No installer, no setup, no admin prompt.

A round OptiDrop bubble appears in the bottom-right corner of your screen.
Drag a game folder onto it, pick your frame generation, and click Install.
Drag the same folder on again and click Remove to undo it.

In game:  Insert = OptiScaler menu   End = frame gen on/off
          Page Up = FPS overlay      Page Down = cycle FPS overlay

(Make sure you extracted the zip first. Running it from inside the zip won't work.)

Full guide: README.md, or https://github.com/kennethwaldmer-ship-it/optidrop
